Introduction

One vision. Many ways to make a difference.

I believe design extends far beyond buildings. It shapes our experiences, influences our opportunities, and determines how we connect with one another and the world around us.

Through practice, entrepreneurship, education, and community, I work at the intersection of the built environment, human potential, and African creativity.

Each initiative I build explores a different part of this vision, from creating thoughtful spaces and meaningful products to expanding access to knowledge and helping people discover their potential.

Different disciplines. One enduring purpose: to build a more thoughtful, inclusive, and life-giving world.

Behind the projects.

International Event Center+
01 / CIVIC / CULTURAL

Enugu, Nigeria

Scope
Event venue with two halls, a central lobby, retail and social spaces.
Design challenge
Expand Enugu’s event capacity while allowing large gatherings to run independently.
Approach
Separate event flows meet in a generous arrival space. Shading, layered façades and daylight openings address heat and comfort.

Garden Home+
02 / RESIDENTIAL

Kigali, Rwanda

Scope
Two-storey home with terraces, gardens and locally sourced natural materials.
Design challenge
Connect everyday living with nature while responding to heat and humidity.
Approach
Open living areas extend outdoors. Shaded glazing, cross ventilation and planting connect both levels to the landscape.

Pine Towers+
03 / MIXED-USE RESIDENTIAL / COMMERCIAL

Enugu, Nigeria

Scope
Residential prototype combining apartments, co-working, a café and wellness spaces.
Design challenge
Accommodate greater urban density without sacrificing comfort or local identity.
Approach
Shared amenities support vertical living. Recessed openings, façade fins and an open landscaped base support shade, airflow and activity.

About Crystal

An architect by training. A builder of ideas by nature.

My work moves between the built environment, creative enterprise, education, and research. Architecture provides one lens through which I understand the world, but my interests extend into the many ways people create, learn, organize, and thrive.

I am drawn to work that bridges disciplines, connects people to opportunities, and brings thoughtful ideas into practice.

Across my ventures and independent work, I continue to explore what becomes possible when design is treated not simply as a profession, but as a way of thinking, creating, and contributing to society.

This is the thread connecting everything I build.
