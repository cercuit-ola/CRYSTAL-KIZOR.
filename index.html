Behind the projects.

International Event Center+
01 / CIVIC / CULTURAL

Enugu, Nigeria

Scope
Event venue with two halls, a central lobby, retail and social spaces.
Design challenge
Expand Enugu’s event capacity while allowing large gatherings to run independently.
Approach
Separate event flows meet in a generous arrival space. Shading, layered façades and daylight openings address heat and comfort.
Crystal’s role
Design Director, Studio COKA. Her project-specific responsibilities have not yet been confirmed.
Read the studio’s project brief ↗
Garden Home+
02 / RESIDENTIAL

Kigali, Rwanda

Scope
Two-storey home with terraces, gardens and locally sourced natural materials.
Design challenge
Connect everyday living with nature while responding to heat and humidity.
Approach
Open living areas extend outdoors. Shaded glazing, cross ventilation and planting connect both levels to the landscape.
Crystal’s role
Design Director, Studio COKA. Her project-specific responsibilities have not yet been confirmed.
Read the studio’s project brief ↗
Pine Towers+
03 / MIXED-USE RESIDENTIAL / COMMERCIAL

Enugu, Nigeria

Scope
Residential prototype combining apartments, co-working, a café and wellness spaces.
Design challenge
Accommodate greater urban density without sacrificing comfort or local identity.
Approach
Shared amenities support vertical living. Recessed openings, façade fins and an open landscaped base support shade, airflow and activity.
Crystal’s role
Design Director, Studio COKA. Her project-specific responsibilities have not yet been confirmed.
